1. Adaptive Windbreak Matrix (AWB-01)
 * Geometric Lattice: Semi-permeable Voronoi tessellation nodes that flex independently under dynamic wind loads, converting turbulent kinetic energy into localized thermal friction across the ground anchor grid.
 * Anchoring Substrate: Pneumatic root-spikes driven by internal micro-actuators that lock into dirt, rock, or concrete surfaces to prevent structural displacement during high-velocity gusts.
 * Thermal-Skin Layer: Embedded light blooms on the windward face that absorb ambient solar radiation and reradiate a stable micro-climate envelope on the lee side.
2. Scavenging Node Interface (SNI-04)
 * Ingestion Port: Wide-mouth, variable-aperture intake lined with diamond-like carbon (DLC) shear teeth for shredding plastics, e-waste chassis, and mixed scrap directly at the collection site.
 * Sorting Matrix: Integrated eddy-current separators and electrostatic deflection plates that instantly route ferrous metals, non-ferrous conductors, and polymer fractions into separate high-pressure consolidation chambers.
 * Telemetry & Sync Beacon: Encrypted local mesh transceiver that updates the central bootstrap scheduler on feedstock volume and remaining battery capacity.
3. Active Air-Filtration Grid (AAF-02)
 * Electrostatic Pre-Filter Array: High-voltage ionization wires charged by the swarm's local energy storage plates to trap airborne particulates, smoke, and industrial aerosols instantly upon contact.
 * Catalytic Micro-Pore Core: Graphene-oxide mesh infused with titanium dioxide nanoparticles that break down volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and ambient pollutants via solar-catalytic oxidation.
 * Laminar Flow Injector: Swarm nodes dynamically reposition around the working perimeter to pull contaminated air through the filtration core and push out a clean, temperature-regulated laminar air curtain around the zone.
